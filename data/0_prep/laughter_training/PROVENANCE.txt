PROVENANCE GAP (documented honestly)
  The script that ORIGINALLY trained clf_A/B/C is NOT in 03_REVISION/scripts/
  (only loaders 08c/09a/11 and different dumpers 02/08b exist) — it was an
  interactive step that was not saved. The v2 train_laughter_classifier.py is a
  RECONSTRUCTED trainer: exact hyperparameters recovered from the pickled model
  (get_params) + the 86-feature extractor preserved verbatim in 08c/09a + the two
  manual CSVs above.

  The reconstruction DOCUMENTS the training procedure but does NOT regenerate the
  model. Measured 2026-08-02 (`--verify`): retraining agrees with clf_C_both.pkl on
  only 94.44% of the training TRs, despite identical hyperparameters, random_state
  and feature extractor — the original was fitted under a different library build.
  clf_C_both.pkl is therefore AUTHORITATIVE: it produced all 280 carried annotations
  and every number in the paper. train_laughter_classifier.py will NOT overwrite it
  (--force writes a side file and reports the agreement instead).

  The INFERENCE path is exact: running the carried model through predict_laughter.py
  reproduces the annotations bit-identically (verified on 6 episodes across 4 seasons,
  100% label match).

Feature extractor : scripts/0_prep/laughter_features.py  (86-dim, shared by
                    train_laughter_classifier.py and predict_laughter.py).
